About the Project

Project Description

This project is an exploratory project to find a proper fit for our Contact Center solution. There is a preference for Contact Center as a Service solutions, but it is not limited to that. Initially, a Request for Information (RFI) will be produced by the team and sent to several vendors selected based on previous interactions and market research (i.e. Gartner). Responses will be evaluated by the team with the goal of shortlisting no more than 5 solutions. The selected solutions will then be sent a Request for Proposal (RFP) which will lead to more detailed discussions and eventually selecting a preferred solution to be presented to the executive team.

Desired Results

The scope of this project is to evaluate the current state of our Contact Center solution and identify areas for improvement. The project will focus on the following areas:

  • Voice Solution
  • Omni Channel Solution
  • Agent Experience
  • Customer Experience
The desired result is to have a clear understanding of the current state of our Contact Center solution and to have a list of potential solutions that can address the identified areas for improvement. The final deliverable will be a report that includes the findings and recommendations for the preferred solution.

This Project is Not

This is an analysis not an implementation project. No development should be done unless the team decides a Proof of Concept is necessary, and if so, it should only be one of the short-listed solutions and limited to 1 - 2 weeks. The exception to this is getting some hands-on experience with the recommended solution prior to the executive recommendation.

Project Team

  • Core Team: Enterprise Architect, Project Manager, Voice Solution Architects, Contact Center Solution Architects, Gartner Analyst and potentially others to be pulled in briefly for certain topics.
  • Informed: Directors of the Voice and Contact Center teams, VP and Senior VP of the Voice and Contact Center teams. Architects from other teams that might be impacted or have an interest (TBD).

Project Timeline

The project is expected to take approximately 3 - 4 months to complete. The timeline is as follows:

  • Month 1: RFI sent to vendors and responses evaluated.
  • Month 2: RFP sent to shortlisted vendors and responses evaluated.
  • Month 3: Final evaluation and recommendation presented to executive team.

Success Criteria

The final deliverable will be a report that includes the findings and recommendations for the preferred solution. The report will include the following:

  • Current State Analysis
  • Identified Areas for Improvement
  • Potential Solutions
  • Evaluation of Potential Solutions
  • Recommended Solution
