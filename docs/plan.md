#  Contact Center Modernization Plan

## 1 Form core team  - Executives and Project Manager - 1 week
## 2a Develop Request for Information (RFI) for all vendors - Solution Architects - 1 month  
## 2b Research vendors to put together a target list - Enterprise Architect and Solution Architects - 1 month (parallel with 2a)
## 3 Evaluate vendors via Q&A sessions regarding their RFI responses - Core Team 1 month
## 4 Create Request for Proposal (RFP) - Core Team - 2 weeks
## 5 Shortlist 5 vendors to submit RFP to and send it out - Core Team - 1 week
## 6 Evaluate RFP responses with Q&A sessions, demos and detailed discussions - Core Team - 1 month
## 7 Select the preferred solution and create a presentation for executives - Core Team - 1 week

# Risks
## 1 Resource Availablity - Mitigate using management to eliminate roadblocks or reassign resources
## 2 Market Changes - Mitigate by working with industry analysts like Gartner to make sure we are getting the latest and vetted  information about the vendors solutions
## 3 Missing Timeline - Accept that it could take longer than expected as the quality of the decision is more important than finishing on time.
