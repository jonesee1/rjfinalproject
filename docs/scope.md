# Contact Center Modernization Analysis

## Purpose
    The purpose of this project is to perform due diligence and vet technologies and vendors to provide a modern Contact Center.  There are two main drivers for this are:
        1 Our Voice solution is aging, and the vendor is in constantly in financial troubles.
        2 Our Omni Channel solutions are not cohesive, causing a suboptimal Agent and Customer Experience. 
    The goal is to find a solution that provides a seamless Omni Channel solution allowing Agents and Customers to use their Channel of choice (voice, chat, self-service etc.) and be able to switch between those channels without losing context.  

## Description
    This project is an exploratory project to find a proper fit for our Contact Center solution.  There is a preference for Contact Center as a Service solutions, but it is not limited to that.  Initially, a Request for Information (RFI) will be produced by the team and sent to several vendors selected based on previous interactions and market research (i.e. Gartner).  Responses will be evaluated by the team with the goal of shortlisting no more than 5 solutions.  The selected solutions will then be sent a Request for Proposal (RFP) which will lead to more detailed discussions and eventually selecting a preferred solution to be presented to the executive team.

## Desired Results
    The desired results:
        - Come out with a solid understanding of the Contact Center's Omni Channel needs.
        - Understand options in the market using vendor discussions, consulting resources and feedback from similar Customers of the vendors.
        - Create a compelling solution presentation for executives illustrating what solutions were examined and the process to reach a final recommendation including the vendor RFI and RFP scoring process.  

## Exclusions
    This is an analysis not an implementation project.  No development should be done unless the team decides a Proof of Concept is necessary, and if so, it should only be one of the short-listed solutions and limited to 1 - 2 weeks.  The exception to this is getting some hands-on experience with the recommended solution prior to the executive recommendation. 

## Feedback Loop
    Directors of the Voice and Contact Center teams, VP and Senior VP of the Voice and Contact Center teams.  Architects from other teams that might be impacted or have an interest (TBD).

## People Resources
    Enterprise Architect, Project Manager, Voice Solution Architects, Contact Center Solution Architects, Gartner Analyst and potentially others to be pulled in briefly for certain topics.

## Priorities
    1 Quality - Selecting a solution that meets the needs and will stand the test of time.
    2 Time - Ideally this is completed within 3-4 months.
    3 Consensus - The entire team must agree on the proposed solution and gain executive support.  

## Acceptance Criteria
    Core team agreement and executive approval of the recommendation constitute the acceptance criteria.
