Contact Center Modernization Analysis

Project Overview

The purpose of this project is to perform due diligence and vet technologies and vendors to provide a modern Contact Center. There are two main drivers for this are:

  • Our Voice solution is aging, and the vendor is in constantly in financial troubles.
  • Our Omni Channel solutions are not cohesive, causing a suboptimal Agent and Customer Experience.
The goal is to find a solution that provides a seamless Omni Channel solution allowing Agents and Customers to use their Channel of choice (voice, chat, self-service etc.) and be able to switch between those channels without losing context.
